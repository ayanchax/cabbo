# Fare & Charges Policy

This policy explains how Cabbo fares and extra charges may work. The exact fare shown for a booking depends on trip type, route, vehicle, timing, availability, and Cabbo's active pricing configuration.

## Fare Estimate

Cabbo may show an estimated fare before booking. The estimate may include known charges available at the time of booking.

The final payable amount may change if trip details change, waiting time increases, route changes, extra distance or hours are used, or additional charges apply during the ride.

## Common Fare Components

A Cabbo fare may include:

- Base fare
- Distance charge
- Time charge
- Vehicle category charge
- Platform fee
- Convenience fee, where applicable
- Driver allowance, where applicable
- Taxes or government charges, where applicable
- Advance payment and balance amount

## Advance And Balance

Cabbo may ask customers to pay an advance amount online to confirm a booking. The remaining balance may be payable to the driver.

The app or support team will show or explain the payment split where applicable.

## Extra Charges

Extra charges may apply for:

- Tolls
- Parking
- State permits
- Airport entry or parking charges
- Night charges
- Waiting time
- Extra kilometers
- Extra hours
- Route changes
- Additional stops
- Luggage or add-ons
- Special pickup or drop requirements

Some charges may be payable directly to the driver if they are not collected online by Cabbo.

## Outstation And Hourly Rides

Outstation and hourly rental rides may have package limits, such as included days, included kilometers, included hours, day allowance, night allowance, or driver allowance.

If the ride exceeds the package limits, extra kilometer, extra hour, extra day, waiting, night, or allowance charges may apply.

## Airport And Permit Charges

Airport parking, entry fees, tolls, permits, and similar charges are often controlled by third parties or authorities. These charges may change without Cabbo control and may be added to the final payable amount.

## Pay-To-Driver Items

Some items may be paid directly to the driver, depending on the booking and location. Cabbo may show these as pay-to-driver, excluded, or additional charges.

Some charges are included in the Cabbo fare when they can be estimated or collected online. Other charges may be payable directly to the driver because they depend on actual route, parking, toll booth, permit counter, airport rules, waiting time, or usage during the ride.

Customers should ask for details if a charge is unclear before paying.

## Fare Changes

Cabbo may update fares, fees, packages, discounts, and charges from time to time. The applicable fare for a booking will be based on the pricing shown or confirmed for that booking, subject to any changes caused by route, time, extra usage, or additional charges.

## Disputes About Charges

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact Cabbo support with:

- Booking ID
- Amount charged
- Payment method
- Screenshot, if available
- Reason you believe the charge is incorrect

Cabbo will review booking details, payment records, trip distance/time, and available driver/support inputs and get back to you.
