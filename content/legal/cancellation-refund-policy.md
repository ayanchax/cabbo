# Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy explains what happens when a customer cancels a booking, a driver is unavailable, a customer is not present, or a payment/refund needs review.

Exact cancellation windows, deductions, and refund percentages should match Cabbo's backend policy configuration.

## Customer Cancellation

You may cancel a booking from the app or by contacting Cabbo support.

Depending on the trip type, pickup time, driver assignment, and cancellation window, Cabbo may apply:

- Full refund
- Partial refund
- Cancellation fee
- No refund of specific non-refundable charges

Any applicable deduction will be shown or explained based on Cabbo's active policy for that booking.

## Cancellation Window

Cabbo may use different cancellation windows for different ride types, such as airport transfers, local hourly rentals, outstation rides, and scheduled rides.

For V1, the final window and deduction rules should be filled from the backend cancellation policy. Example fields may include:

- Free cancellation until a defined time before pickup.
- Partial refund after the free cancellation window.
- Higher deduction close to pickup time or after driver dispatch.
- No refund for customer no-show after the allowed waiting period.

## Driver Or Cabbo Cancellation

If Cabbo or the assigned driver cannot fulfil a confirmed booking due to driver unavailability, vehicle issue, operational constraint, or other Cabbo-side reason, Cabbo may offer:

- Reassignment to another driver or vehicle
- Rescheduling where practical
- Full refund of eligible customer payment
- Support-led resolution based on the situation

## Customer No-Show

A no-show may apply when the driver reaches the pickup location, waits for the allowed waiting time, tries to contact the customer, and the customer is unavailable or does not start the ride.

In a no-show case, Cabbo may deduct cancellation, waiting, driver dispatch, or other applicable charges from the paid amount.

## Late Driver Or Delayed Pickup

If the driver is delayed, contact Cabbo support. Depending on the delay and ride status, Cabbo may help coordinate, reassign, reschedule, cancel, or review refund eligibility.

Traffic, weather, road closures, police restrictions, vehicle breakdowns, and network issues may affect pickup time.

## Refund Source

Approved online payment refunds are usually sent back to the original payment source through the payment partner.

For payments made directly to the driver, refund or adjustment handling may differ and may require support review.

## Refund Timeline

Once Cabbo initiates an eligible refund, settlement time depends on the payment method, bank, card network, UPI provider, wallet, or payment gateway.

Cabbo may show an estimated refund timeline, but actual credit to your account may be controlled by the payment partner or bank.

## Failed Or Delayed Refunds

If a refund fails or is delayed, Cabbo may need to verify the payment gateway status, refund ID, bank response, and settlement details before taking the next step.

Contact support with your booking ID and payment reference if the expected settlement time has passed.

## Non-Refundable Or Deductible Charges

Some charges may be non-refundable or deductible depending on the booking and policy, including:

- Platform fee
- Payment gateway charges, where applicable
- Driver dispatch cost
- Cancellation fee
- Waiting charges
- No-show charges
- Already incurred tolls, parking, permits, or taxes

The final list should follow Cabbo's active pricing and cancellation policy.

## Support Path

For refund questions, contact Cabbo support with:

- Booking ID
- Payment reference
- Cancellation time
- Screenshot or bank message, if available
- Short explanation of the issue
