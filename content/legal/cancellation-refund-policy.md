# Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy explains what happens when a customer cancels a booking, a driver is unavailable, a customer is not present, or a payment/refund needs review.

Refund decisions are based on Cabbo's backend cancellation policy for the trip type and service region or state.

## Customer Cancellation

You may cancel an eligible booking from the app.

When a customer cancels, Cabbo marks the trip as cancelled, records the cancellation reason, clears any remaining balance payment for that trip, and releases the assigned driver if one was already assigned.

Refunds for customer cancellations apply only to eligible advance payments collected online. The balance amount is not collected by Cabbo after cancellation.

Depending on the trip type, pickup time, service region or state, and active cancellation policy, Cabbo may apply:

- Full refund of the eligible advance payment.
- Partial refund of the eligible advance payment.
- No refund of the advance payment.

## Cancellation Window

Cabbo may use different cancellation windows for airport pickup, airport drop, local, and outstation rides.

For customer cancellations, Cabbo checks the configured free-cancellation cutoff for that trip type and service region or state.

- If the booking is cancelled before or at the free-cancellation cutoff, the eligible advance payment is refunded in full.
- If the booking is cancelled after the free-cancellation cutoff but before the scheduled trip start time, Cabbo applies the configured partial refund percentage to the advance payment.
- If the booking is cancelled after the scheduled trip start time, no automatic refund is applied.
- If no active cancellation policy is configured for that trip type and service region or state, Cabbo may not be able to initiate an automatic refund and the case may require support review.

The displayed policy text is only an explanation. Cabbo's backend cancellation policy is the source of truth for refund eligibility and refund amount.

## Driver Or Cabbo Cancellation

If Cabbo, a driver, or Cabbo operations cancels a confirmed booking due to driver unavailability, driver cancellation, driver no-show, vehicle issue, operational constraint, or another reason that is not customer cancellation or customer no-show, Cabbo treats the cancellation as Cabbo-side for refund purposes.

In such cases, Cabbo may offer reassignment, rescheduling, support-led resolution, or full refund of the eligible online advance payment.

## Customer No-Show

A customer no-show may apply when the driver reaches the pickup location, waits for the allowed waiting time, tries to contact the customer, and the customer is unavailable or does not start the ride.

If a booking is cancelled as customer no-show, the advance payment is not eligible for refund.

## Late Driver Or Delayed Pickup

If the driver is delayed, use the Call or WhatsApp support option on the booking detail page. Depending on the delay and ride status, Cabbo may help coordinate, reassign, reschedule, cancel, or review refund eligibility.

Traffic, weather, road closures, police restrictions, vehicle breakdowns, and network issues may affect pickup time.

## Refund Source

Approved online payment refunds are usually sent back to the original payment source through the payment partner.

For payments made directly to the driver, refund or adjustment handling may differ and may require support review.

Cabbo currently refunds only eligible online advance payments through the payment provider. Amounts payable directly to the driver are not refunded through Cabbo's online refund workflow.

## Refund Timeline

Once Cabbo initiates an eligible refund, settlement time depends on the payment method, bank, card network, UPI provider, wallet, or payment gateway.

Cabbo may show an estimated refund timeline, but actual credit to your account may be controlled by the payment partner or bank.

## Failed Or Delayed Refunds

If a refund fails or is delayed, Cabbo may need to verify the payment gateway status, refund ID, bank response, payment settlement status, and settlement details before taking the next step.

Use the support option on the booking detail page with your booking ID and payment reference if the expected settlement time has passed.

If a refund cannot be initiated immediately because the payment has not settled or the payment provider does not return the required details, Cabbo may retry or review the refund through internal processing.

## Non-Refundable Or Deductible Charges

Some charges may be non-refundable or deductible depending on the booking and policy, including:

- Platform fee
- Payment gateway charges, where applicable
- No-show charges
- Already incurred tolls, parking, permits, or taxes, if any

Cabbo does not automatically deduct separate cancellation charges from the advance payment outside the configured refund percentage. The final refund amount follows Cabbo's active backend cancellation policy.

## Support Path

For refund questions, use the support option on the booking detail page and keep the following details ready:

- Booking ID
- Payment reference
- Cancellation time
- Screenshot or bank message, if available
- Short explanation of the issue
