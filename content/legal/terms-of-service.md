When you place a booking, you must provide correct pickup, drop, trip type, date, time, passenger, and contact details.

Cabbo is focused on scheduled rides, not instant street-hailing. Driver assignment may happen closer to the pickup time. Cabbo may contact you by phone, WhatsApp, SMS, email, push notification, or in-app message for booking updates.

Cabbo may decline, cancel, or modify a booking where required due to driver availability, safety concerns, incorrect details, suspected misuse, payment issues, operational constraints, or events outside Cabbo's reasonable control.

## Customer Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- Being available at the pickup location on time.
- Keeping your phone reachable before and during the ride.
- Sharing accurate trip details.
- Treating drivers, Cabbo staff, and other passengers respectfully.
- Paying all applicable charges.
- Following lawful and safe conduct during the ride.

You must not carry illegal, unsafe, hazardous, or restricted items in the vehicle.

Some bookings may have a balance amount or pay-to-driver amount. You are responsible for paying the balance, tolls, parking, permits, waiting, overage, and other applicable charges that are shown during booking or become payable during the ride.

If a payment is successful but the booking is not confirmed, Cabbo will verify the transaction and either confirm the booking, issue a refund where applicable, or guide you through the next step.
