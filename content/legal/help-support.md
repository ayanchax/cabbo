# Help & Support

Cabbo support helps customers with bookings, payments, cancellations, refunds, driver coordination, and ride-related questions.

## For Booking Help

For trip-specific help, open your booking detail page and use the Call or WhatsApp support option shown there.

Please keep your booking ID ready. It helps us find your ride faster and avoid repeat questions.

Trip support availability may depend on operating hours and team availability. If a request cannot be handled immediately, Cabbo will review it as soon as the team is available.

## Common Reasons To Contact Us

- You need help creating or changing a booking.
- You have not received a booking confirmation.
- Your driver details are not visible yet.
- You want to cancel a ride.
- You need help with a payment, refund, or failed transaction.
- You want to report an issue after a completed ride.
- You need an invoice or booking details.

## Before The Ride

For scheduled rides, Cabbo may assign the driver closer to the pickup time. If your driver is not assigned yet, check the booking status on the booking detail page.

If your pickup time is close and you cannot reach the driver, use the Call or WhatsApp support option on the booking detail page.

## During The Ride

For route, pickup, drop, or coordination issues, first speak with the driver where practical. If the issue is not resolved, use the Call or WhatsApp support option on the booking detail page.

In an emergency, contact local police, ambulance, or emergency services first. Cabbo support is not a substitute for emergency services.

## After The Ride

If something went wrong during the ride, use the support option on the booking detail page as soon as possible and keep these details ready:

- Booking ID
- Date and time of ride
- Pickup and drop locations
- Driver or vehicle details, if available
- Short description of the issue
- Any relevant screenshots or payment references

## Lost & Found

Cabbo will reasonably assist customers in coordinating with drivers regarding lost items. However, Cabbo cannot guarantee recovery of lost items.

## Response Expectations

Response times vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request. Some issues, such as refunds, payment checks, driver verification, or route review, may take longer because they require confirmation from payment partners, drivers, or internal records.

## Abuse

Cabbo may refuse abusive, threatening, or fraudulent support requests.

## Support Channels

For trip support, use the Call or WhatsApp support option shown on the booking detail page. For your safety, do not share OTPs, passwords, session access details, full card details, or other sensitive information with anyone claiming to represent Cabbo.
